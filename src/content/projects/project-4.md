Monitoring of transactional systems through **Grafana** dashboards and **Airflow** pipelines, with alerting on SLA compliance and direct issue resolution when thresholds were breached.

Remote **SQL** operations against production replicas and **SMPP** server interventions for SMS-billing flows. Incident triage handled through **Nagios** alerts and **Freshdesk** ticketing.

Automated repetitive operational tasks and documented runbooks so first-line support could resolve common incidents without escalation. Coordinated with internal teams and mobile-network operators for end-to-end issue closure within the SENDIA business unit.
